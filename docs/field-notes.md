The IDIM / iTEC water prepayment system is a project by the Innovative Design & Integrated Manufacturing Lab. at Seoul National University. The goal of the project is to develop a water prepayment system that uses solar power. The system will have three main components: a hardware controller, a communication module, and a software program.

The hardware controller will be responsible for reading the RFID card, controlling the motor valve, and communicating with the LoRa module. The communication module will be responsible for sending the water usage data to the Hope solar power plant. The software program will be responsible for managing the user accounts and tracking the water usage.

The project is currently in the development phase. The team has already completed the PCB design and soldering. They are now working on configuring the wires and connecting the Molex connectors. The next step is to test the operation of the RFID, valve, and OLED.

The following are the problems that the team has encountered so far:

* The step-up module soldered on the PCB is not connected well.
* The wires are not configured correctly.
* The connections are not secure.

The team has created a list of tasks to address these problems. These tasks include:

* Adding lead to the step-up module to make a good connection.
* Configuring the wires according to the PCB markings.
* Connecting the Molex connectors by soldering Molex cables in the order marked on the PCB.
* Connecting the wires suitable for each position to the relay and checking the operation (RFID, Valve, OLED).

The team is also bringing the following tools and materials to the village:

* M4 and m2 hex wrench for screws
* Small screw driver
* Soldering machine and lead
* Molex cable and connector
* Multimeter
* Power bank
* Laptop (Arduino)
* 5 pin cable
* Extra aruduino Nano 33 IoT

The team will continue to work on the project and will update the schedule as needed.
